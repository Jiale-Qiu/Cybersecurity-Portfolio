# National Cyber League

## 『 ❓ About the NCL 』

The National Cyber League, or the NCL, is a biannual cybersecurity competition for highschool and college students in the United States and Canada. Seeing around 7000 contestents per season, The competition covers a wide range of cybersecurity competencies ranging from offensive engagements to defensive investigations.
